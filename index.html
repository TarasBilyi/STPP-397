A puzzle adventure rooted in creativity

ReGarden Match combines classic match-3 puzzle solving with garden restoration and personalization. Every completed level rewards players with resources used to renovate different areas of a once-neglected garden — unlocking fountains, statues, pathways, and blooming flowerbeds. The tone should feel gentle, rewarding, and imaginative, appealing to players who enjoy both puzzle challenges and creative design.
